SEMAGLUTIDE
A GLP-1 receptor agonist with FDA-approved active ingredient. Reduces appetite by acting on hunger centers in the brain, slows gastric emptying, improves insulin sensitivity, and supports sustained fat loss — particularly visceral and subcutaneous fat. Most common side effects are transient and GI-related (nausea, bloating, constipation). The most established GLP-1 option with the longest safety track record. Key distinction from Tirzepatide: targets only GLP-1 receptor — Tirzepatide adds GIP agonism for stronger metabolic effect.

TIRZEPATIDE
A dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist with FDA-approved active ingredient. The first molecule to simultaneously activate both incretin receptors. Consistently outperforms semaglutide in weight loss magnitude in head-to-head trials. Produces greater fat loss relative to lean mass, improves triglycerides and HDL, and has strong evidence for obesity, type 2 diabetes, metabolic syndrome, NAFLD, and sleep apnea. Similar GI side effect profile to semaglutide. Best choice for clients whose primary goal is body composition transformation.

RETATRUTIDE
A triple GLP-1/GIP/glucagon receptor agonist — FDA Phase 3. The most advanced metabolic agent in development. Glucagon receptor activation increases energy expenditure (thermogenesis), a mechanism absent in semaglutide and tirzepatide. Phase 2 trials showed up to 24% body weight loss over 48 weeks — exceeding any currently approved agent. Long-term safety data still emerging. For clients seeking the frontier of metabolic transformation.

SERMORELIN
A synthetic GHRH analogue (first 29 amino acids). Stimulates the pituitary to produce its own GH in a physiological, pulsatile pattern — preserving the body's natural feedback loop. Supports deep sleep quality, lean muscle, fat reduction, recovery, energy, and mood. Well tolerated; most common side effect is mild injection-site reaction. The most conservative and natural GH-stimulating option. Key distinction from CJC-1295: shorter half-life, lower-amplitude GH pulses — ideal for clients new to GH optimization.

NAD+
A coenzyme essential for cellular energy production, DNA repair, and sirtuin activation. NAD+ declines significantly with age. Powers mitochondrial energy (electron transport chain), activates longevity-associated sirtuins (SIRT1-SIRT7), supports DNA damage repair, reduces oxidative stress and inflammation, and may support cognitive function and cardiovascular resilience. Generally well tolerated; some clients experience transient flushing or warmth. Included in every Auryx protocol because all other peptides and metabolic interventions work more effectively in a cell with sufficient NAD+ to power its repair and signaling machinery.

CJC-1295 + IPAMORELIN
Auryx's most popular GH stack. CJC-1295 is a GHRH analogue that triggers GH release; Ipamorelin is a selective GHRP that amplifies pulse amplitude without raising cortisol or prolactin. Together they act synergistically. Produces strong physiological GH pulses particularly during sleep, restores deep slow-wave sleep, supports lean muscle and fat loss, improves recovery and IGF-1 levels, and enhances skin, energy, and vitality over 3-6 month protocols. Ipamorelin is notably selective — does not raise cortisol, prolactin, or hunger hormones. Key distinction from Tesamorelin: broader GH optimization — better sleep, lean muscle, general anti-aging. Better choice for men seeking overall GH optimization vs. targeted visceral fat reduction.

TESAMORELIN
FDA-approved active ingredient (indicated for HIV-associated lipodystrophy as Egrifta). The only GHRH-class peptide with full FDA approval. Clinically proven reduction of visceral adipose tissue (VAT) — the metabolically dangerous fat surrounding internal organs. Elevates IGF-1, supports lean body composition, may improve cognitive function, and supports cardiovascular risk markers. Most common side effects: mild joint discomfort, fluid retention, injection-site reactions. Key distinction from CJC + Ipamorelin: superior for visceral fat reduction with strongest clinical evidence in this category, but slightly less impact on broader GH spectrum (sleep, lean muscle). The Tesamorelin + Ipamorelin stack combines both mechanisms.

PT-141 (BREMELANOTIDE)
A synthetic melanocortin receptor agonist. FDA-approved for hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD) in premenopausal women (Vyleesi); used via compounding in men for libido and erectile function. Acts centrally on the brain (MC3R and MC4R receptors) — not peripherally on vasculature, distinguishing it from PDE5 inhibitors. Increases sexual desire and arousal in both men and women. In men, supports erectile function through a central mechanism. Onset 45-60 minutes, effects last several hours. Most common side effects: transient nausea, flushing, mild blood pressure increase. Not recommended for clients with cardiovascular disease or uncontrolled hypertension. Key distinction from sildenafil/tadalafil: works on desire and arousal through the brain — effective even when PDE5 inhibitors have been insufficient.
