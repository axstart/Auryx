IPAMORELIN (SOLO)
A selective growth hormone releasing peptide (GHRP). Unlike older GHRPs, selectively stimulates GH release without raising cortisol, prolactin, or ghrelin — making it exceptionally well tolerated for long-term use. Improves sleep quality (particularly slow-wave deep sleep), supports lean muscle and fat reduction, enhances recovery, and improves skin quality and energy. Excellent tolerability — most common side effect is mild transient water retention. Key distinction from CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin: solo ipamorelin produces more modest GH pulses. When paired with CJC-1295, the combination is significantly stronger than either alone. Solo ipamorelin is ideal for clients seeking a gentle entry into GH optimization or who want to add pulse amplification to an existing GHRH protocol.

GHK-Cu (COPPER PEPTIDE)
A naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide found in human plasma whose concentration declines with age. Stimulates collagen and elastin synthesis, promotes wound healing and tissue remodeling, activates antioxidant and anti-inflammatory pathways, supports hair follicle health, and may reduce hair loss. Excellent safety profile with decades of use in cosmetic and wound-care applications. Unique dual role as both a regenerative and anti-aging peptide with particularly strong evidence in skin and hair applications. Core component of the Auryx GLOW Complex.

THYMOSIN ALPHA-1
A 28-amino acid peptide naturally produced by the thymus gland. One of the most clinically validated peptides in this catalog — approved in over 35 countries for infectious disease and cancer immunotherapy indications. Modulates and strengthens T-cell mediated immunity, enhances dendritic cell function, supports NK cell activity, and reduces chronic low-grade inflammation. Excellent safety record across decades of clinical use globally. Key distinction: acts as an immune modulator, not an immune suppressant — helps the immune system respond more intelligently. The most evidence-backed immune peptide in the Auryx catalog.

EPITHALON
A synthetic tetrapeptide derived from a natural pineal gland extract. Studied for over four decades in longevity science. Activates telomerase — the enzyme responsible for maintaining telomere length and associated with cellular aging. Restores and regulates circadian rhythm via pineal gland support, supports melatonin production and sleep quality, modulates neuroendocrine function, and demonstrates antioxidant properties. Well tolerated in available studies. Key distinction: unique telomerase-activating mechanism makes it one of the only peptides studied for direct impact on cellular aging at the chromosomal level. Often paired with NAD+ for complementary anti-aging mechanisms.

SEMAX
A synthetic heptapeptide derived from ACTH fragment 4-7 with no hormonal activity — acts purely as a neurotrophin modulator. Increases BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor) and NGF expression, improves focus, attention, and working memory, supports mood stability and stress resilience, and demonstrates neuroprotective effects in models of stroke and TBI. Well tolerated with no significant adverse effects at standard doses. Key distinction from Selank: Semax is primarily a cognitive enhancer and neuroprotectant — elevates focus, neuroplasticity, and BDNF. Selank is primarily anxiolytic. They are complementary and often used together in CNS optimization protocols.

SELANK
A synthetic heptapeptide analogue of the endogenous peptide tuftsin. Approved in Russia as an anxiolytic and nootropic. Unlike benzodiazepines, reduces anxiety without sedation, dependence, or cognitive impairment. Modulates GABAergic, serotonergic, and dopaminergic neurotransmission, improves memory consolidation, supports stable mood and emotional regulation, and demonstrates immune-modulatory properties. No withdrawal or dependence reported. Key distinction from Semax: Selank is the anxiolytic — primary strength is reducing anxiety and improving emotional regulation without cognitive dulling. Semax is the cognitive enhancer. They complement each other and are often used together in CNS optimization protocols.
