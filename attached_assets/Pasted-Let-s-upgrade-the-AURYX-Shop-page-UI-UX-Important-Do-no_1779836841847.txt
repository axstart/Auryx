- Obsidian / charcoal accents

- Elegant product staging
- Mobile-first UX

Avoid:
- Cheap supplement store look
- Generic product grid feel
- Harsh black boxes behind every product
- Tiny product images
- Cluttered cards
- Aggressive medical claims
