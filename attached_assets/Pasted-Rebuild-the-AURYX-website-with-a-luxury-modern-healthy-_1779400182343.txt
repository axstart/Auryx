Rebuild the AURYX website with a luxury, modern, healthy lifestyle-oriented aesthetic.

Brand direction:
AURYX is a premium precision wellness and peptide education brand. The site should feel like a luxury longevity, recovery, vitality, and lifestyle optimization platform, not a generic supplement store or research chemical catalog.

Visual style:
Use an elegant editorial design with generous spacing, refined typography, and a premium color palette:
- Obsidian black / deep charcoal as the primary background
- Warm ivory / soft stone for light sections
- Champagne gold as the accent color
- Optional muted sage, olive, or warm taupe as secondary lifestyle accents

Typography:
Use an elegant serif font for headlines, such as Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or a similar refined editorial serif.
Use a clean modern sans-serif for body copy, such as Inter, Satoshi, or Helvetica-style typography.
Headlines should feel luxurious and calm, not aggressive.

Design improvements:
- Remove the generic product-grid feeling from the homepage.
- Avoid repeating the same vial image across all product cards.
- Use larger editorial lifestyle imagery.
- Use soft gradients, subtle borders, premium cards, and spacious layouts.
- Make the site feel like a high-end wellness destination.
- Avoid clutter, tiny text, overpacked cards, and overly dark unreadable sections.
- Mobile design must feel premium and easy to read.

Homepage structure:

1. Hero section
Headline:
“Precision wellness, delivered with intention.”

Subheadline:
“AURYX offers education-first peptide protocols designed to support performance, recovery, vitality, skin health, and healthy aging. Built for people who want to feel sharper, move better, and age with strategy.”

Primary CTA:
“Find Your Protocol”

Secondary CTA:
“Explore Protocols”

Small supporting line:
“No hype. No shortcuts. Just modern wellness, clear guidance, and a more intentional way to optimize.”

Hero visual:
Use an editorial luxury wellness image or abstract premium visual with soft gold particles, subtle motion, and clean composition. Avoid aggressive gym/bodybuilding imagery.

2. Protocol categories section
Title:
“Choose your area of focus.”

Create six elegant cards:
- Metabolic Support
- Recovery & Resilience
- Skin & Healthy Aging
- Energy & Vitality
- Cognitive Performance
- Sleep & Restoration

Each card should have a short, compliance-conscious description using “support” language, not medical claims.

3. Concierge quiz section
Title:
“Your protocol starts with your rhythm.”

Body:
“Answer a few questions about your goals, lifestyle, and current routine. We’ll guide you toward the AURYX protocol category that best matches your priorities.”

CTA:
“Start the Protocol Finder”

Make this look like a premium concierge intake experience, not a basic form.

4. The AURYX Standard
Create four pillars:
- Quality-vetted sourcing
- Education before action
- Lifestyle-first optimization
- Discreet, elevated experience

Use refined iconography, thin lines, and premium spacing.

5. Lifestyle section
Title:
“Optimization is not a shortcut. It is a standard.”

Body:
“Peptides are only one part of the equation. The AURYX philosophy connects modern wellness with daily rhythm: training, sleep, nutrition, recovery, and consistency.”

Use a luxury lifestyle image: sauna, morning routine, refined gym, skin care, recovery, or modern wellness environment.

6. Product preview section
Title:
“Explore the protocol collections.”

Show selected product cards, but make them feel editorial. Each card should include:
- Product name
- Protocol category
- Short support-focused description
- “Explore product” button

Do not use strong medical claims, disease claims, guaranteed outcomes, or before-and-after language.

7. Education section
Title:
“Learn before you optimize.”

Create article cards:
- “What are peptide protocols?”
- “How recovery shapes long-term performance”
- “Why healthy aging starts with rhythm”
- “The difference between support and shortcuts”

8. Final CTA
Headline:
“Build your protocol with intention.”

Body:
“Start with your goals. Understand your options. Choose a more elevated path to modern wellness.”

CTA:
“Find Your Protocol”

Footer:
Include links for About, Protocols, Education, Contact, Terms, Privacy, Disclaimer.

Compliance:
Add a clear disclaimer in the footer:
“This website is for educational purposes only and does not provide medical advice. Products and information are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Consult a licensed healthcare provider before starting any protocol.”

Tone:
Luxury, calm, intelligent, modern, trustworthy, lifestyle-oriented.
Avoid hype, aggressive sales copy, medical promises, or cheap supplement language.
