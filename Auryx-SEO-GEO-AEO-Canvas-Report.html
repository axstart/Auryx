Findings by priority

PriLensIssueDetail
P0SEOSPA routes 404 in production No rewrite to index.html. Live checks: /shop, /learn, /blog, product & blog URLs return NOT_FOUND. Only / works. Sitemap points crawlers at dead URLs.

What is already working

  • Homepage baseline: title, description, robots, canonical, OG/Twitter, MedicalBusiness + FAQ JSON-LD
  • Learn page: MedicalWebPage + FAQPage, physician author/reviewedBy, strong educational copy
  • Blog posts: unique metaDescription, Article JSON-LD, faqAnswer on H2s, named MD author
  • robots.txt allows crawl and points to sitemap; admin disallowed
  • OG image 1200×630 present and live
