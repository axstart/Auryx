High-level remaining work

Module A — Infrastructure & security

In place: encrypted site, staff login. Gaps: two-step admin login, staging site.

  • Review domain, delivery network, and certificate setup and tighten what is already in place.
  • Add firewall, abuse, and bot protection that fits the current host.
  • Require two-step login for staff and admin accounts.
  • Stand up a separate staging environment that mirrors production.
  • Turn on uptime and error alerts, plus a backup and recovery plan.
  • Limit admin access so people only see what their role needs.

Module B — SEO & search

In place: sitemap and crawl file. Gaps: inner pages not findable; analytics not connected.

  • Fix public pages so Google and shared links open shop, Learn, Journal, and product URLs.
  • Finish the technical search audit: titles, headings, redirects, and structured data.
  • Improve mobile experience and page speed on key pages.
  • Connect Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and purchase tracking.
  • Complete foundational keyword and competitor research for organic search.

Module C — Protocol Finder measurement

The Finder stays. The gap is measurement, not a new quiz.

  • Keep the current Protocol Finder flow and recommendations unchanged.
  • Track each step, drop-offs, and completions inside the Finder.
  • Track product views, add-to-cart, and checkout from Finder and shop.
  • Deliver a clean funnel report the business can use for optimization.

Module D — COA verification MVP

Certificates exist on products. The contract asks for a lot-number lookup, not a supply-chain platform.

  • Map each product batch / lot number to its Certificate of Analysis.
  • Publish a simple public lookup page for verification.
  • Use this as the LegitScript-facing MVP only — no warehouse or manufacturing rebuild.

Growth & customer automation

Reporting and journeys sit on the existing customer and order records. Messaging costs stay with the client.

Reporting layer

  • Customer lifetime value from existing orders.
  • Repeat-purchase and purchase-frequency views.
  • Simple marketing cohorts and segments — without replacing the database.

Email & SMS journeys

  • Audit the current Zoho email setup.
  • Welcome sequence for new customers.
  • Cart and checkout abandonment recovery.
  • Post-purchase follow-up and win-back flows.

Left unchanged by design

Bottom line

The website is past “under construction.” Customers can already find products, complete Protocol Finder, check out, and download certificates. The contract work is the next layer: harden the platform, make the site searchable, measure the Finder-to-purchase path, add a lot-number COA lookup, and turn existing customer data into reporting and automated follow-up.
